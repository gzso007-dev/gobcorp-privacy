GobCorp Privacy Policy

Developer: Kealon · Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game GobCorp (package com.gobcorp.idlefactory) collects, why, and who it is shared with. GobCorp is a single-player idle game. It has no account system of its own and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

1. Game progress stored on your device

Your factory, research, upgrades and settings are saved locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up with Google Play Games (see below). Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Google Play Games (optional cloud save)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress is stored as a saved game in your own Google account so you can restore it on another device. Sign-in and saved games are provided by Google and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Google password or email address. You can delete saved games from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them on reinstall.

4. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. Ads are provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Ads). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity Ads may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and language, and how you interact with ads. Ads are personalized only if you allow it: the game asks the first time you open it, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Cloud › Personalized Ads. Otherwise Unity Ads shows non-personalized ads. See the Unity Privacy Policy for details and your options.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not run our own servers that collect your data, and the game contains no analytics or tracking of its own beyond what the services above perform.

6. Children

GobCorp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Because the game keeps your data on your device or in your own Google account, you can delete it directly as described above. For data held by Google or Unity, use the controls and contact details in their privacy policies. For anything else, contact us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
